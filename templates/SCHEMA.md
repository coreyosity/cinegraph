# Cinegraph note schemas

The vault is the single source of truth and doubles as the Quartz `content/` root.
Two invariants make the graph work in **both** Obsidian and Quartz:

1. **Frontmatter properties are plain text** (queryable by Bases). Relationships are
   expressed as `[[wikilinks]]` in the note **body** — neither Obsidian nor Quartz
   treats links inside YAML as real links.
2. **Body link text == target note basename**, exactly. Names are normalized once via
   `common.link_name()` (strip reserved chars + trailing dots), and that same string is
   used for the filename and the wikilink. Quartz is case-sensitive and does not
   normalize, so any drift produces a broken "Untitled" 404 node.

## Film note — `Films/<Title>.md`

```markdown
---
type: film
title: Blade Runner 2049
year: 2017
tmdb_id: 335984
rating: 4.5            # user's Letterboxd rating (0.5–5.0), preserved on re-runs
watched: 2023-05-12    # preserved on re-runs
runtime: 164
genres: [Science Fiction, Drama]
director: Denis Villeneuve
studios: [Alcon Entertainment, Warner Bros.]
cast: [Ryan Gosling, Ana de Armas, Harrison Ford]
keywords: [dystopia, artificial intelligence]
poster: https://image.tmdb.org/t/p/w500/...jpg
tags: [film]
---

Director: [[Denis Villeneuve]]
Cast: [[Ryan Gosling]] · [[Ana de Armas]] · [[Harrison Ford]]
Studios: [[Alcon Entertainment]] · [[Warner Bros]]
Genres: [[Science Fiction]] · [[Drama]]
```

`rating` / `watched` originate from Letterboxd and are **never** overwritten by
enrichment. Entity names in the body are the normalized `link_name` form (note
`[[Warner Bros]]` although the frontmatter keeps the raw `Warner Bros.`).

## Entity stub — `People/<Name>.md`, `Studios/<Name>.md`, `Genres/<Name>.md`

Generated by `scripts/gen_entities.py`. Body is intentionally minimal — Obsidian and
Quartz auto-render the **backlinks** (every film that links here), which is the hub page.

```markdown
---
type: person          # or: studio | genre
roles: [actor, director]   # people only; merged across films
tags: [person, actor, director]
---

# Denis Villeneuve
```

People live in a **flat `People/`** folder (no Directors/Actors split) so a person who
both directs and acts is one note — avoiding duplicate basenames that make `[[Name]]`
ambiguous.
