title: Your recurring ensemble
description: A network of the actors who most often appear together across the films you've watched.
---

The actors who keep showing up *together* in what you watch. Each dot is an actor (bigger =
more of your films); lines connect actors who've co-appeared 3+ times. Drag nodes around,
scroll to zoom, and click a face to jump to their page. Watch for the clusters — franchises
and a director's regular troupe tend to clump.
