A personal map of everything I've watched — films, the people who made them, the studios
behind them, and the genres they live in — built from my Letterboxd history.

The **constellation** above maps my films by similarity (shared director, cast, themes and
genre) — coloured by **taste island**, sized by rating; click a dot to open a film. See the
[[Taste Map]] for the named islands, bridge films and outliers. Or **search** to jump
anywhere and use the **breadcrumbs** to find your way back here.

## Browse

- [[Up Next]] — **what to watch next**: your watchlist ranked by predicted enjoyment, plus suggestions, filtered by what's streaming
- [[Films]] — every film logged
- [[People]] — actors & directors, each linking back to their films
- [[Studios]] — the studios behind the films
- [[Genres]] — films grouped by genre
- [[Themes]] — recurring themes & motifs (grief, heist, one-shot…) across your films
- [[Watchlist]] — everything still unwatched (the full list)
- [[Discover]] — the full gallery of taste-mined recommendations
- [[Stats]] — at-a-glance charts: ratings, decades, top directors/actors/genres/studios
- [[Ensemble]] — the network of actors who recur together across your films
- [[Tag graph]] — your diary tags (who you watch with, where, how) wired into clusters
- [[Taste Map]] — your constellation charted into named islands, bridges & orphans

## Stats

The [[Stats]] page has live charts computed in the browser. For deeper interactive
slicing (Obsidian only), the Bases views:

- [Films](/_bases/films.base) — all films, top-rated, by decade, by director
- [People](/_bases/people.base) — top actors, top directors, actor-directors
- [Studios](/_bases/studios.base) — studios by film count
- [Genres](/_bases/genres.base) — genres by film count
- [Themes](/_bases/themes.base) — themes ranked by your rating
- [Watchlist](/_bases/watchlist.base) — what's still unwatched
