---
title: Tag graph
description: A network of your diary tags — the people, places, and platforms that get logged together.
---

Every tag from your film logs, wired to the tags it's logged *alongside*. Each dot is a tag
(bigger = more films); lines link tags that co-occur, thicker = more often. The colours mark
the clusters the graph finds on its own — your cinema crew, your solo nights, family trips —
without anyone labelling them. Drag nodes around, scroll to zoom, and click a tag to open its
page.
